# Zhuhan Jin

Interdisciplinary quantitative researcher working across data science, AI/ML, econometrics, behavioral science and computational modeling. PhD candidate in Civil and Environmental Engineering at the National University of Singapore (expected January 2027); MPhil in Economics, University of Oxford.

My research models how people make decisions and how social networks influence them. I use fine-tuned language models and graph variational autoencoders to generate synthetic populations, Bayesian spatial models to measure peer effects, and agent-based simulation and mixed-integer optimization to test scenarios. I work mainly in Python (PyTorch, PyTorch Geometric, Hugging Face Transformers).
